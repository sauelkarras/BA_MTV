Pointwise contradictions

Premise ⇒ conclusion on two attributes.
Numeric example: attr1 > 70 and target attr2 = retired gives attr1>70=>attr2=retired.
Categorical example: attr7 = A75 and attr6 ≠ A65 gives attr7=A75=>attr6!=A65.
